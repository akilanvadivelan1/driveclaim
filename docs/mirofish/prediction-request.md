# Prediction request for MiroFish

Paste the text between the lines into the "prediction requirement" box.

---

Loopd is a driving game for iPhone where players drive loops in their cars to claim land, score points by square mile, and steal land from each other. The attached brief describes exactly how it works.

Simulate the first three months after a small public launch. Assume about 2,000 players in one mid-size American metro area, mixed commuters and hobbyists, with a friends feature and a national leaderboard. Treat these assumptions as adjustable.

Please predict and explain:

1. Who adopts the game first, who drops out, and what week-by-week retention looks like.
2. How stealing land plays out between friends and between strangers. Does it create rivalry, or harassment and quitting?
3. Whether the points race pushes anyone toward unsafe driving, and how players, local media and parents would talk about it.
4. What privacy complaints appear when land shapes reveal where people live and drive.
5. How cheating appears and how fast it spreads once there are visible rankings.
6. What a new player with no friends nearby experiences.
7. Which two or three rule or feature changes would most improve the game, and which would most reduce the risks above.

End with a short list of the strongest and weakest points of the design.
