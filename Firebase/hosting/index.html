Drive a loop. Claim the land.

Loopd is an iPhone game. The area inside every loop you drive becomes your territory, worth 1,000 points for each square mile. Close a loop over a friend's land and the overlap becomes yours.

Ask for a beta invite

Free. iPhone with iOS 16 or later. For ages 13 and up.

The rules are short

Only new land scores. Drive the same loop twice and the second one is worth nothing. Loops smaller than about 0.02 square miles, which is 20 points, do not count.

The overlap goes to the driver. When you end a drive, your route is closed with a straight line back to where it began. The land inside is yours, and anyone whose land you crossed loses exactly that shape.

A server checks every drive. It looks at speed, jumps in the GPS signal and gaps in the route, so faking land does not work.

Friends, state and nation

Share an invite link or an eight character code. Friends see each other's land on a map, tap it to see whose it is, and compete on a friends board. There are also boards for your state and for the whole country.

Every row shows points, square miles and minutes driven.

Built for the road, and for privacy

Hands free. Results are read aloud, so you never need to look at the screen. Set up before you drive, and never touch your phone while the car is moving.

Only the shape is kept. Loopd saves the shape of the land you claim, not your raw GPS points.

Your land is public to other players. It follows the roads you drive, so do not claim land near places you want kept private.

You can leave any time. Delete your account, and everything stored about it, from inside the app. Read the privacy policy.
