Loopd

Drive a loop. Claim the land. Loopd is an iPhone game where the land inside every loop you drive becomes yours, and every square mile is worth 1,000 points. Invite friends, take land from each other, and climb the leaderboards.

Please do not use your phone while driving. Set up before you start and let the voice updates do the work. Loopd is for ages 13 and up.
