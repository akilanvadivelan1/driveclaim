Loopd privacy policy

Last updated: November 2026

Loopd is a driving game. You drive a loop in your car, the land inside the loop becomes yours, and you earn points for every square mile. This page explains what Loopd collects, who can see it, and how you can delete it.

Who we are

Loopd is made by Akilan Vadivelan. You can reach us at akilanvadivelan1@gmail.com.

Who can use Loopd

Loopd is for people who are 13 or older. The first time you open the app it asks if you are 13 or older. If you say no, the app stops there. If we learn that someone under 13 has an account, we delete it and its data.

What we collect and why

  • Email address. Only if you create an account. We use it to sign you in, verify that the address is yours, and reset your password. Your password is handled by Google's Firebase Authentication. We never see it.
  • A random ID. Every player, including guests, gets one so the game can tell players apart.
  • Your display name. The name you choose. It is shown to other players.
  • Your location while you drive. When you tap Start drive, the app records GPS points and the time of each one. It uses your location in the background if you allow that, so a drive keeps working with the screen off. When you tap End drive, the app sends those points to our server, which works out the land you claimed and checks that the drive is real. We do not keep the raw GPS points after that. We keep only the shape of the land you claimed.
  • Your land and totals. The shape of each area you claimed, its size, the state it is in, your points, and the total minutes and miles you have driven.
  • Friends. Who you are friends with, and your invite code.
  • On your phone only. Your answer to the age question, and your "Remember me" choice. These never leave your phone.

What other players can see

Loopd is a shared map, so some of what you create is visible to others.

  • Your land is visible to every other signed-in player on the national map. Because it follows the roads you drive, it can show areas you drive in often, which may include where you live or work. If that worries you, do not claim land near places you want to keep private.
  • Your display name, points, square miles and minutes driven appear on the leaderboards. The national board also shows your state.
  • Friends can tap your land to see your name and total points.
  • We never show your email address to other players.

Who else handles your data

  • Google Firebase runs our sign-in, database and server code. It stores and processes your data for us, possibly in the United States or other countries where Google operates.
  • Apple Maps draws the map. Apple's privacy policy applies to the map itself.

Loopd has no ads and no analytics tools. We do not sell your data, and we do not track you across other apps or websites.

How long we keep it

We keep your data until you delete your account. We may keep it longer only if the law requires us to.

Deleting your account

In the app, open Friends, then Account, then Delete account. This permanently removes your sign-in, your name, your land, your points, your leaderboard entries, your friend connections and your invite code. It cannot be undone. Copies in any system backups are removed on the normal schedule of our provider. You can also email us and we will do it for you.

Your choices

  • You can turn location off for Loopd at any time in your iPhone's Settings. The game cannot track a drive without it.
  • You can play as a guest without giving an email address. Guest progress lives only on that phone and is lost if you delete the app.
  • To see, correct or remove your information, email us.

Safety

Data is sent over encrypted connections, and the database only lets the app read what it needs. No system is perfectly secure. Please do not use your phone while driving. Set up the game before you start, and let the voice updates do the work.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top. If a change is significant, we will tell you in the app.
